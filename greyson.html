Greyson: Brand New 2021 Mercedes Benz Sprinter

Greyson (super creative name for a grey themed van, I know) is a sultry, luxury build featuring a modern color-way complete with matte-grey walls, Japanese Cedar ceilings, black quilted fabrics and black hardware delicately set off by bronze highlighted furnishings to give this van a cozy, yet stunning interior you'll never want to get out of. While a full list of features lies below, Greyson's highlights include my signature Hideaway Shower, rear outdoor shower (both with hot water), under-bed storage a plenty with a pull out table and removable center cabinets to allow large items such as surfboards or skis, a hot water heater, extra large Dometic fridge / freezer combo and an extended bed space, giving dwellers over 72" of sleeping length, giving you all the amenities needed for today's vanlife. Add a multi-function roof rack, side ladder, Fiamma awning and a super neat-o wind fairing and Greyson is the king of the road... or camping spot... or driveway. It's just really cool, ok?

When it comes to construction and materials robust is a must. With 1/2" Maple ply walls, 3/4" subfloor and 3/4" ceiling planks, Greyson's shell is built for the long haul. With a solid base in place nearly every other piece of furniture inside is built with 3/4" Maple plywood and coated with eco friendly water-based polyurethane for true longevity. Accompanied by solid butcher block bench and countertops, the highest quality luxury vinyl plank flooring and load bearing drawer slides for peace of mind.

Feeling a little dirty? An outdoor shower provides fresh, warm water to rinse off the dust and dirt of an amazing day in nature. Feeling a little shy? Simply flip open the countertop to reveal a 24" square shower pan with luxurious walls. Throw up the shower curtain, grab the extra long sink faucet and wash your dirty bits in the comfort of your home on wheels. It should be noted that the shower pan is also the perfect place for your portable toilet. Just swing open the doors and use as needed.

Is that all? Of course not. Please take the time to look through the photos, examine the details and message with any questions. Thanks for looking!
