Ethel

1965 GMC Handi-Van

Watch as we slowly bring our show piece, Ethel, to life.

Build Highlights

Audio

Custom Panels. Infinity Sound. Retro Styling.

We scoured the internet to find premium quality Infinity speakers to match the retro styling of Ethel.

Hidden Technology

Custom wood dash.

With a desire to hide any modern functionality without actually giving it up, we've designed this wood dash to house an in-line volume control as well as a secret way to hold a smartphone and display maps, music and more.

The Basics

From the ground up.

We gutted this lady down to the bolts. And then, we scrubbed. With a fresh canvas, the process begins.
