Meet Foley

Just like Meals On Wheel but, with art! It may not feed your belly but it will absolutely gorge your eyes and force feed your soul with anglehair lines and demi-glace finishings. A testament to a termites version of a Micheline Star meal... on wheels.

Ok, I'm done... or not to be done... Ok, ok. With free reign over the detail in this design we couldn't have been more hungry to dive in and spend our time mulling over every painstaking detail. Custom wood coverings for every surface. Hand routed vents in all mechanical boxes. Two-tone stain schemes leaving revealed edges and vents a vidid natural Maple while the faces rendered in whitewash to match. Art Deco inspired transitional coverings, Walnut butcher block throughout and a hand shaped coat rack to boot. Foley, as our customer named him, is certainly ready for a night on the t... trails.

Featuring our one of a kind hidden shower, a custom fridge box that doubles as a bench and one of the most robust electrical systems we've done to date, Foley is the complete package for any van dweller.
