Tanner

Sold

Tanner (I know, I know, another creative wonder of nomenclature) is an open concept, modern camper built for indoor congregation and outdoor exploration alike. Throughout the build you will find a host of natural woods showcasing phenomenal grain structure from responsibly sourced lumber. Set off by luxurious black stained cabinetry, with just a hint of transparency to let you know it's the good stuff, Tanner is pure eye candy. Combine that with luxury hardware and a truly decadent backsplash and a high-end sound system, this little home on wheels is much more than just a place to rest your head.

Featuring a dinette style bed that easily morphs between sleeping for two and dining for 6, this build also accommodates sleeping for a third camper with a hidden slide-out, making Tanner perfect for small families and guests... Ok, maybe don't bring Aunt Sue. Sorry Aunt Sue but, you snore and think the woods are "yucky." Featuring my proprietary Hideaway Shower and an outdoor shower (both with hot water), a spacious front access fridge / freezer, overhead storage galore and a utilitarian roof system with a stylish steel fairing, this luxury home on wheels feels spacious and grande while giving you the modern amenities needed for an adventurous life on the road.

When it comes to construction and materials robust is a must. With 1/2" Maple ply walls, 3/4" subfloor and 3/4" ceiling planks, Tanner's shell is built for the long haul. With a solid base in place nearly every other piece of furniture inside is built with 3/4" Maple plywood and coated with eco friendly water-based polyurethane for true longevity. Accompanied by solid butcher block bench and countertops, the highest quality luxury vinyl plank flooring and load bearing drawer slides for peace of mind.

Feeling a little dirty? An outdoor shower provides fresh, warm water to rinse off the dust and dirt of an amazing day in nature. Feeling a little shy? Simply flip open the countertop to reveal a 24" square shower pan with luxurious walls. Throw up the shower curtain, grab the extra long sink faucet and wash your dirty bits in the comfort of your home on wheels. It should be noted that the shower pan is also the perfect place for your portable toilet. Just swing open the doors and use as needed.

Is that all? Of course not. Please take the time to look through the photos, examine the details and message with any questions. Thanks for looking!
